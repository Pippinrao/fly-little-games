MMPX 2x shader port
===================

This shader is a FlyNES GLSL ES 2.0 semantic port of the official MMPX reference by
Morgan McGuire and Mara Gagiu. The port was independently verified against frozen expected
pixels generated by the official JavaScript implementation.

Paper: https://www.jcgt.org/published/0010/02/04/paper.pdf
Official supplement: https://www.jcgt.org/published/0010/02/04/supplement.zip
Supplement SHA-256: 1211c4b59d5bea3c5ebeb1dc3b31fe8a2f0c58f64955cee50e939f96543996e1
